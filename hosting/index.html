Access and records

Separate Organizations, Defined Access.

Organization scope, staff permissions and customer access are different controls.

Organization scope
Business records belong to an organization. Database row-level security policies use the organization context to restrict access to tenant records.
Staff permissions
Roles and privileges govern the actions a person may perform. Administrative, operating and platform-support responsibilities have distinct access controls.
Customer portal
Customers use a separate portal account to access their authorized sites, equipment, requests and released documents. Portal access is scoped separately from internal staff access.
Final documents
Final documents retain their issued content and verification information. Corrections are recorded through a superseding document so the earlier issued record remains available.
