From Demonstration to Adoption

Plan the Pilot and Rollout.

The proposal sets responsibilities, acceptance criteria and a schedule for these five steps.

  1. 01

    Demonstrate the Fit

    Bring an equipment list and a representative workflow. Demonstrate the relevant product and identify what needs evaluation.

  2. 02

    Set the Pilot Scope

    Agree the product, people, sites, workflows, terms and acceptance criteria. Name the owners of configuration, data and review.

  3. 03

    Validate a Migration Sample

    Review an authorized sample export, mappings, relationships and attachments. Resolve exceptions before a wider transfer.

  4. 04

    Accept Roles and Workflows

    Exercise the agreed tasks, permissions and failure cases. Record unresolved requirements before accepting the configuration.

  5. 05

    Complete the Operating Handover

    Agree training, support, updates, backups, restoration and export. Record the acceptance decision and anything still open.

Commercial Terms

Know What the Proposal Covers.

The written proposal defines charges, inclusions and responsibilities. Pricing and package limits depend on the agreed scope.

Price Basis and Inclusions
Specify the product, edition, enabled capabilities and licensing basis, including how users, sites and scope affect the price.
Implementation and Import Assistance
Assign data preparation, mapping, configuration and validation. State what assistance is included and what costs extra.
Training and Support
Define operator and administrator training, support contacts, escalation, service hours and any response commitments.
Acceptance and Next Steps
Name the acceptance owner and MetraSlate contact. Record the scenarios, results and limitations behind the adoption decision.

Migration and Record Acceptance

An Equipment Import Is a Starting Point.

The current CSV and XLSX import creates new equipment records. It does not establish complete migration of historical service records, business relationships or attachments from another system.

Prepare the Sample
Your data owner selects representative records, including incomplete or conflicting examples. Define the identifiers, field meanings and source documents before mapping them.
Inspect the Result
Check record counts and identifiers, then open sampled records and their linked attachments. Verify that relationships and document associations retain their intended meaning. Record anything that needs manual work or remains outside the import scope.
Plan the Wider Transfer
Resolve the sample findings before scheduling a larger migration. Confirm source retention, access during the transition, reconciliation and who authorizes the final changeover.

Hosting and Operating Ownership

Decide Who Operates Each Part.

Assess the hosting arrangement for the selected product. The proposal must confirm feasibility, requirements and service commitments.

MetraSlate-Hosted Arrangement

Confirm the Hosted Scope.

Ask MetraSlate to identify the environment, data location and application services it would operate. Define your administrators’ responsibilities for accounts, access and day-to-day use.

Confirm Before Adoption
Application and infrastructure ownership, monitoring, update coordination, support, backup policy and restore responsibility.

Customer Infrastructure

Assess the Environment First.

Bring your infrastructure requirements and technical owner for a feasibility review. A discussion of this arrangement is not confirmation that self-hosted delivery is available for every product or configuration.

Confirm Before Adoption
Supported infrastructure, installation and maintenance ownership, access for support, update compatibility and recovery procedures.
Backups and Restore
Name the backup operator and restore owner. Set coverage, frequency, retention, recovery objectives and a restoration check. Service levels require an explicit agreement.
Export and Exit
Specify exportable records, relationships, files and formats, plus any limits or charges. Validate the export and retain required records before ending access.
