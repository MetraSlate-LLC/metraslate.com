Deployment

Cloud Hosted, or Yours.

Choose the model that fits how you have to run.

Cloud hosted
The platform operated by MetraSlate, for businesses that want it without owning the infrastructure underneath.
Self-hosted
The same platform, deployed on hardware you own, for businesses whose regulations or preference keep the records on their own premises. Standard is a single application over a PostgreSQL database.

Isolation

Each Business is Its Own Tenant.

Enforced in the database, not only in the application.

Row-level security
Every tenant table carries a policy in the database itself. A connection with no tenant sees zero rows, not everybody’s. A mistake elsewhere in the application cannot bypass it.
Sealed documents
Documents a business issues are frozen when final, cryptographically signed, and append-only: a superseding document is a new record, never an overwrite.
Portal wall
Customer portal accounts live behind a wall of their own and cannot reach the business’s doors, and business accounts cannot use the portal’s.
