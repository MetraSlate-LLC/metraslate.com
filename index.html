Why Standard

What You Get with Standard.

  1. The Whole Job in One System

    A job runs from the first request to the paid invoice without leaving Standard. Each order shows where every item on it stands, from received to in the work, finished and shipped.

  2. Your People Work in the Same Place

    Each person opens Standard on their own day: what needs them, the work they carry and what is coming. The team talks in channels and direct messages beside the work they are talking about.

  3. Your Customers Follow Their Work

    Each customer has one page for their equipment, their open work and their documents, and a portal of their own that shows the same work from their side.

  4. You See How the Business is Doing

    The books are kept from the work itself. The invoice a job raises is the revenue on the income statement, and the month closes in the same system.

  5. The Routine Runs Itself

    Say what should happen when something happens, and Standard does it. When an item is received, worked, finished or shipped, it moves to its next stage without anyone touching it.

Where We Started

Where We Started: Calibration.

The Metrology Edition gives a calibration lab its full depth: Standard made for the labs that check measuring instruments read correctly.

OmniCal Suite is the calculators and worksheets that go with it, or work on their own.

  1. Every Point on One Sheet

    An OmniCal Suite run puts every test group on one sheet, with the readings, the limits, the expanded uncertainty and the TUR of every point.

  2. Every Number Shows Its Work

    Open any point and see how its uncertainty was calculated: the scope band, the standard uncertainty, the root sum of squares, the expanded uncertainty and the TUR.

  3. Capability You Can Show

    Build a CMC budget from its contributors at both ends of a range, then send it to Datasheet Creator as the band it supports.

Metrology Edition: Standard for calibration labs, the labs that check measuring instruments read correctly.

Asset

Asset is software for keeping track of a company’s equipment and running the business around it. It records what each item is, where it is and what has been done to it, next to the company’s customers, purchasing, staff and invoices.

Asset comes with Standard as the equipment and business foundation it runs on.

OmniCal Suite

OmniCal Suite is a set of calculators and worksheets for people who calibrate measuring instruments, that is, check that they read correctly. It builds the worksheet for a calibration and works out how sure each result is, showing every step of the math. It also works out the size of thread gages, the plugs and rings used to check screw threads.
