Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition

A Calibration Job, From Request to Invoice.

A customer sends a pressure gauge for calibration. The team needs to agree the service, receive the instrument, perform the measurements, review the evidence and deliver the result.

  1. 01

    Request and Estimate

    Record the customer’s requirements, prepare service lines and retain the approval.

  2. 02

    Receive and Assign

    Identify each instrument, record receipt and give the work a responsible team member.

  3. 03

    Measure and Evaluate

    Use approved templates and reference standards. Prepare CMC budgets. Record readings, uncertainty and guardbanding.

  4. 04

    Review and Issue

    Review the technical record and produce the certificate with its retained measurement basis.

  5. 05

    Serve the Customer

    Give authorized customers access to equipment, service progress and released certificates.

  6. 06

    Invoice and Understand Cost

    Follow invoices and balances. Review revenue, cost of service and operating expenses.
