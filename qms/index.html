Nonconforming work

Follow the Whole Investigation.

Contain the problem, understand its cause and record the result of the fix.

  1. 01

    Record and Contain

    Record the finding, its severity and source. Describe containment and link the equipment or work affected.

  2. 02

    Investigate the Cause

    Build a cause tree, follow the chain of whys and name the root cause. Keep the reasoning and supporting evidence with the finding.

  3. 03

    Act and Communicate

    Assign corrective actions and due dates. Record affected-item dispositions and whether the customer must be notified.

  4. 04

    Check Effectiveness

    Record whether the corrective action worked. Closure checks keep unresolved actions, undecided affected items and owed customer notices visible; laboratory policy governs root-cause and effectiveness requirements.

Controlled information

Know Which Revision the Work Used.

Procedures and revisions
Keep controlled procedures, their revisions and approval history together. Retained revision copies let a reviewer inspect historical instructions rather than assuming the current document was always in force.
Datasheets and measurement policy
Structure test points, tolerances and formulas in datasheets. Service levels bring uncertainty, decision rules, guardband and rounding policy to the calibration.
Certificate templates
Use Document Creator to author reusable, data-bound certificates. Publish template versions separately from the review and release of individual customer certificates.

Traceability and audit evidence

Show the Basis Behind the Result.

Frozen calibration records
Completed events retain point results, uncertainty components, standards, exclusions and supporting snapshots. Database controls prevent application updates or deletion of those completed records.
A correction preserves its predecessor
A corrected calibration is a new event linked to the earlier event. The original result remains part of the history.
Separate review and release
Technician signing, review approval and sealing are distinct recorded actions. Follow the technical evidence and the decisions that supported a certificate.
Affected work
Review the instruments and work connected to a quality finding, record their dispositions and track the customer-notification decision.

These controls support your laboratory’s quality system and evidence review. Accreditation and independent assurance depend on the laboratory’s procedures, configuration and operation.
