Know Your Equipment.
Keep Its History Close.

AssetLight by MetraSlate brings instrument identity, recorded location, due dates and supporting records into a focused equipment workspace. It is for teams that need to maintain their equipment book while keeping broader business operations elsewhere.

From the Register to the Record

Find the Instrument That Needs Attention.

Start with your organization’s equipment, narrow the list by recorded due date, then open the instrument to understand its identity and history.

Identify the Instrument
Use asset number, manufacturer, model and serial number to distinguish one instrument from another. Create a record or update its identifying information and recorded location.
Review What Is Due
Filter for overdue equipment, equipment due within 30 days, or records with no due date. The current due-date workflow concerns calibration; confirm any broader maintenance requirements in your pilot.
Follow Recorded Changes
Review service events, field changes and status history with the instrument. These records help explain what changed; physical movements still need the appropriate recorded event.
Find Supporting Evidence
Open available certificates from the equipment record and see which external calibration entries still need a document.
Discuss Your Equipment Requirements

Create Records or Bring an Equipment List

Check the Import Before It Becomes Your Register.

The current CSV and XLSX import creates new equipment records. Use the template, preview the file and review validation findings before committing the import.

Prepare a Representative File

Start with the equipment identifiers, site and location your team maintains. The current import accepts up to 500 data rows and 10 MiB per file.

Resolve Rejected Rows

Check validation findings for identities, status and company sites. Correct the file and validate it again before committing; an import with rejected rows does not create equipment records.

Reconcile the Result

Review the import receipt and the resulting equipment records. Plan existing-record updates, historical documents and any larger migration separately with the pilot team.

Discuss Your Equipment Import

Keep the Document with Its Event

Make Service Evidence Easier to Find.

An instrument’s history and its supporting certificate answer related questions. Keep the service event identifiable, then file the document against the right entry.

File the First PDF
The current document workflow attaches the first PDF to an existing external calibration event. Check the instrument, provider and recorded service date before attaching it.
Retain the Recorded Meaning
Attaching a PDF does not extract its results or change service and due dates. Confirm how those details are entered and reviewed as part of your pilot.
Keep Ownership Clear
Your standalone equipment workspace is separate from a service provider’s customer portal. Establish the equipment and records shared for a specific service relationship; your whole inventory is not automatically shared.

Full Asset Includes the Light Foundation.

Light focuses on equipment management. Full Asset includes that foundation and general business operations such as customer work, purchasing, people and invoicing. Standard develops the foundation around an industry’s workflows.

If your needs expand, discuss the move to full Asset. Plan how equipment, files, users, permissions and history will be retained, and verify the transition with representative records during your pilot.
