The Equipment Book

Find What Needs Attention.

Identify
Find equipment by asset number, manufacturer, model or serial number. Maintain its identifying information and recorded location.
Plan Service
Filter overdue equipment, items due within 30 days and records without a due date. These filters currently concern calibration; broader maintenance needs require a scope discussion.
Review History
Follow recorded service, field changes and status history. Physical handovers require their own recorded events.
Keep the Evidence
Open available certificates and identify external calibration entries that still need a document.

Import and Documents

Check the Records You Bring In.

Preview a CSV or XLSX file, resolve validation findings and review the import receipt.

New Equipment
The current import creates new records, with up to 500 rows and 10 MiB per file. Rejected rows must be resolved before any equipment is created. Existing-record updates and larger migrations need separate planning.
External Certificates
Attach the first PDF to an existing external calibration event. Uploading a document does not extract results or change service and due dates.
Ownership
Your inventory remains separate from a provider’s customer portal. Define which equipment and records belong in each service relationship.
