Proposed Service Journey

Make the Request Useful to Both Sides.

A useful request identifies the equipment, ranges, service needs, documents and timing. A useful offer makes scope, turnaround, price, exclusions and logistics clear.

The owner would compare the complete offer and confirm the agreement with the chosen provider. Retained service history would give the next request a starting point.

Participation and Product Scope

Keep the Service and Business Roles Clear.

Participation limits, commercial terms and provider onboarding remain to be defined. No free tier or launch date is promised here.

Asset manages the equipment owner’s broader business. Standard Metrology supports the laboratory’s calibration and business operations. The proposed marketplace would connect owners with providers.
