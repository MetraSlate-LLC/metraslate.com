The proposed experience

Scope before price.

The buyer needs to compare the whole job: service capability, turnaround, exclusions, shipping, and the documents supplied. These are planned workflows.

  1. 01

    Define the requirement

    Prepare the equipment identity, measurement functions, ranges, location, and required date.

  2. 02

    Identify suitable providers

    Review capabilities and confirm the exact scope with a laboratory or supplier.

  3. 03

    Compare the agreement

    Consider the complete quote, including exclusions, timing, logistics, and documentation.

  4. 04

    Keep the service context

    Retain the accepted scope and service reference with the equipment history.
