From Search to Service

Start With the Equipment You Need Serviced.

A search for an instrument, measurement range or calibration service should lead to a useful page. Metrologistics is designed to connect that discovery with relevant lab capabilities and a clear path to an estimate or request for quote.

Your Equipment. Your Choice.

Choose the Lab on the Merits of the Job.

Compare the capabilities, turnaround and quote that matter to your equipment. You choose the laboratory and confirm the scope and price before shipping your equipment.

Describe the Requirement
Identify the equipment, measurement ranges, service needs, required documentation and timing. Give laboratories enough context to assess the job and prepare a useful estimate.
Compare Suitable Laboratories
Review relevant capabilities and scope, quoted price, turnaround, exclusions and logistics. Select the provider whose offer fits your requirements.
Confirm the Service and Send the Equipment
Confirm the service, terms and shipping arrangements with your chosen lab. The provider receives the equipment and carries out the approved work.
Bring the History Into the Next Request
Receive the equipment and released service documents. Retained service history gives the next request a starting point and helps a successful lab relationship continue.

The Customer Relationship

A Service Request Has a Life Beyond the Quote.

Equipment receipt, progress, customer decisions and released documents all matter to the owner. The existing Standard customer experience illustrates the relationship that the marketplace is intended to support.

Participation and Product Scope

Join the Service Relationship. Grow With Your Operation.

Free customer participation is intended to make it practical to work with a laboratory. Broader equipment and business management has its own home in Asset and Standard.

Free Customer Participation
The intended scope includes limited assets and limited service history for the customer relationship, without analytics or business insight. Participation does not require ownership of MetraSlate business software.
Asset for Your Own Business
Full Asset brings your organization's equipment, people, customer work, purchasing, invoicing and insight into a shared business system.
Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition for the Lab
The laboratory's operating system connects customer work with calibration, review, certificates and business operations. The lab fulfills the service while the customer follows the relationship.
