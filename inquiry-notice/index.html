What You Send with a Request

The Request a Demo form sends your name, company and email to MetraSlate for review and follow-up, with the product you chose. Phone, what you want to see and how you heard of us are optional, and help us prepare a relevant response.

How We Handle Your Request

Request Handling

The form sends your request to MetraSlate’s own system, where it opens a sales record holding your company and your contact details and notifies a person at MetraSlate. The message you see after sending means the form was sent; it does not book a meeting or confirm email delivery. The address your connection comes from is used to limit repeated requests.

If the Form Cannot Be Sent

The form opens your own mail program with the same details addressed to hello@metraslate.com. Nothing is sent until you send that message yourself.

Keep the Example Appropriate

Describe the workflow without sending credentials, customer records or other sensitive material. Choose a suitable transfer method if your evaluation needs sample files.

Website Analytics

The production site is configured for Cloudflare Web Analytics and checks Global Privacy Control and reduced-data preferences before loading its analytics script.

Questions About Your Details

Contact hello@metraslate.com about an inquiry or how its information is handled.
