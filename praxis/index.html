Metra Praxis · Discovery and development method

Learn the work.
Then define the software.

Metra Praxis turns domain expertise into a defined system and a bounded first release. The people who know the work establish the requirements; the software is judged against those requirements.

  1. 01

    Learn the work

    Interview the people responsible for the roles, records, workflows, standards, and difficult decisions. Use their vocabulary.

  2. 02

    Define the system

    Describe the records and their lifecycles, access, obligations, integrations, and operating scale.

  3. 03

    Review with domain experts

    Check the proposed system against the interviews and evidence. Resolve gaps with the people who know the domain.

  4. 04

    Bound the first release

    Decide what ships first and the acceptance criteria before building it.

  5. 05

    Verify against the work

    Use working-software evidence and review to establish whether the defined scope holds.

The record that matters most

What is the consequence
if this record is wrong?

That question identifies where the design needs its strongest discipline. In a calibration business, the issued certificate and its evidence demand particular care.
