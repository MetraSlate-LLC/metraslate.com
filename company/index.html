The origin

Built from the Standards.

Standard is built by a metrologist, from the discipline itself: ISO/IEC 17025, Z540.3 and the GUM, read as requirements and answered in software. The bench, the QA queue, the backlog and the audit are all in it because those are the documents a calibration lab is measured against, and software that ignores any of them fails the lab at exactly the wrong moment.

That is where the sealed record spine, the exact-decimal arithmetic and the standing rule that nothing ships without measurement come from: the published requirements, worked through from first principles.

MetraSlate is bootstrapped, and we are actively beginning the funding journey. If you run a lab, certify the people who do, back companies like this one, or simply care how this work gets done, we should talk.
