Company

Business Software Shaped by the Work It Serves.

MetraSlate makes software that runs the whole business of a company that does work for its customers. It started with calibration labs, which check that measuring instruments read correctly and prove it with a certificate.

The Family

  • Standard by MetraSlate:

    Standard is software that runs the whole business of a company that does work for its customers. It is the Asset core fitted to the way a specific industry works. It carries each job from the customer’s first request to the paid invoice: the quote, the work order, the work, pickup and delivery, and the invoice. The customer follows the job in their own online portal.

    Metrology Edition: Standard for calibration labs, the labs that check measuring instruments read correctly.

  • Asset by MetraSlate:

    Asset is the core of the MetraSlate platform: the equipment, the customers, purchasing, people and invoices that every business in the family runs on. It records what each item is, where it is and what has been done to it, next to the company’s customers, purchasing, staff and invoices. For companies that own equipment, it is the end-to-end business OS, and it plugs directly into Standard.

    Standard is Asset fitted to the workflows of a specific industry, and every Standard company runs on it.

  • OmniCal Suite by MetraSlate:

    OmniCal Suite is a set of calculators and worksheets for people who calibrate measuring instruments, that is, check that they read correctly. It builds the worksheet for a calibration and works out how sure each result is, showing every step of the math. It also works out the size of thread gages, the plugs and rings used to check screw threads.
