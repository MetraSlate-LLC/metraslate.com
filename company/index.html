The origin

Built from the Standards.

Calibration laboratories need to record measurements, evaluate uncertainty, identify the standards used and preserve the evidence behind an issued certificate. Requirements from ISO/IEC 17025, ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 and the GUM informed the design of these workflows in the Metrology Edition.

The same attention to records supports the work around the measurement: customer requirements, responsibilities, review decisions, delivery and invoicing.

How we work

Metra Praxis.

Read about the method.

Every edition is made the same way: learn the work from the people who do it, write the whole system down, review the picture with the experts, deliver a bounded first release, and verify it against the work. We report; the customer decides.
