Business Software Shaped by the Work It Serves.

MetraSlate builds asset-management and business software for organizations working with instrumentation and equipment. Asset provides the industry‑independent foundation. Standard develops it for particular industries.

The Family

  • Standard by MetraSlate:

    Run a business from lead generation through delivery and accounts receivable. Built for any industry.

    Metrology Edition: the first edition. A calibration job, from request to invoice.

  • Asset by MetraSlate:

    Equipment management and general business operations. AssetLight is the equipment-only part.

  • OmniCal Suite by MetraSlate:

    Calibration datasheets, measurement uncertainty, CMC budgets and thread gage worksheets.
