The origin

Built from the Standards.

Standard was built from the discipline itself: ISO/IEC 17025, ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 and the GUM, read as requirements and answered in software. That is where the sealed record spine, the exact-decimal arithmetic and the rule that a claim carries its evidence come from: the published requirements, worked through from first principles.

Calibration’s proof is a hard one to earn. A system built to satisfy a metrology audit, with exact decimals and sealed records, is a sound foundation for any industry’s work.
