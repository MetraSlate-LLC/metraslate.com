MetraSlate

Built Inside the Industry It Serves.

MetraSlate was founded by a working calibration professional whose career in calibration and metrology started in the Navy and eventually ran through the biggest names in the business. Standard is not a software company's idea of a lab. It is a lab person's answer to tools that were never good enough. Veteran owned. Solely designed: the stack, the schema, and every last detail curated by one set of hands.

Built the Hard Way.

Standard comes from years inside the work: the bench, the QA queue, the endless backlog and the overtime it demands, the inefficient fractured systems that made it that way, the audit. All of it distilled into one platform with a sealed record spine, exact-decimal metrology, and a standing rule that nothing ships without measurement. MetraSlate is bootstrapped, and we are actively beginning the funding journey. If you run a lab, certify the people who do, back companies like this one, or simply know exactly what everyone has settled for, we should talk.

Calibration first

The First Edition Was Always Going to Be This One.

STANDARD is a platform for any industry. Calibration is first because it is home, and because its proof is the hardest to earn: a system that satisfies a metrology audit, exact decimals and sealed records and all, carries any industry's work.

The family is built around that funnel. ASSET delivers world class asset management with a direct plug into the top of the calibration industry's funnel: asset requests route through Metrologistics to the lab that can meet them on merit, and a lab's own portal customers come straight to their lab. Labs stay primary with their customers, capability is always the customer's open door, and work beyond a lab's scope moves through Metrologistics as a network transfer rather than inter-company paperwork.
