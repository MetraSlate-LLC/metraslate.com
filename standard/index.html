Standard

Built for Any Industry.

One system, start to finish: a job runs from the first lead to the paid invoice.

Standard by MetraSlate runs a business from lead generation through delivery and accounts receivable.

Request a Demo

The Platform

From Lead Generation Through Accounts Receivable

Standard is the industry specialization of Asset’s agnostic record and business foundation, adding the workflows, processes and records your industry needs.

Leads and Sales

Take a lead from a web form or enter it by hand, qualify it, and move the opportunity across a pipeline board whose stages you set.

Quotes

Build a quote, send it, revise it, and turn the approved quote into the work.

Work Orders

Coordinate service, receipt, work, technical review and return through the same customer and equipment records.

Pickup and Delivery

Plan pickups and deliveries as stops on a route, with a driver and a driving order. The driver records who signed for each handover.

Invoicing and Accounts Receivable

Raise the invoice from the work order, issue it, record payments against it, and follow receivables and what is overdue.

The Business

What Else It Runs

The records behind the work live in the same system as the work.

Equipment

Use the shared Asset foundation for equipment identity, location, custody, service history and the resources employees need.

Purchasing and Stock

Keep vendors, purchase orders, bills and inventory in the same system as the work.

Rentals

Rent equipment out, check it out and back in, and invoice the rental.

Accounting

A chart of accounts, a journal, a month close, and the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance.

Quality and Record Control

Connect findings, technical evidence, corrective actions, controlled documents and customer consequences.

Workflow Automation

Configure supported responses to operating events.

Analytics and Reporting

Define subjects, measures, periods, filters and groupings, and review the records that contribute to the result.

Your People

Chat, Video and a Store Are Built In

Your people talk about the work in the system that holds the work.

Team Chat

Channels, direct messages, groups and threads, with search, mentions and attachments. A conversation can name the record it concerns and be kept on that record.

Employee Store

Employees order supplies and gear from the company’s own shelf, with prices and what is on hand.

Company Show

Publish video episodes to the whole company or to one chat channel, feature an episode, and see how often each one was played.

Customer Portal

What Your Customers Can Do

Each customer signs in to a portal that shows their own equipment, orders and account.

Request a Quote

Customers choose the equipment and the services, add notes, save a draft and send the request. The business returns a priced quote.

Approve a Quote

Customers approve the quote in the portal, download it, and submit their purchase order number.

Request a Pickup

Customers ask for a pickup at one of their sites with a preferred date, a time window and a note. The request becomes the pickup the business plans, and the customer follows it from requested to collected.

Follow the Work

Each order shows its progress step by step from opened to closed, and each item on the order shows its own stage.

Answer Questions and Decisions

When the business needs an answer before the work can continue, an authorized contact gives it in the portal.

Invoices and Purchase Orders

Customers see each invoice, what is paid and the balance, open the invoice document, and submit a purchase order number.

Message the Business

Customers write to the business from the portal or from the page of a single item. The message opens a conversation with the customer service team.

Equipment and Documents

Customers see their equipment and what is due, and download the documents the business has released.

Store

Customers order from the business’s store, choose shipping or pickup, and the order is billed to their account.
