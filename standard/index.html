A Measurement Needs Its Supporting Record.

Consider a pressure instrument sent for calibration. Its identity and service history provide the equipment context; the calibration adds measurement evidence, technical review and a released record.

Record the Measurement
The technician captures readings with the reference standard, limits and supported uncertainty calculation.
Review the Evidence
The reviewer inspects the retained technical basis. Completing the measurement and approving the record are separate responsibilities.
Release the Result
The issued certificate belongs to the instrument and customer’s service record, with amendment and withdrawal history.
