Standard

Standard is software that runs the whole business of a company that does work for its customers. It is the Asset core fitted to the way a specific industry works. It carries each job from the customer’s first request to the paid invoice: the quote, the work order, the work, pickup and delivery, and the invoice. The customer follows the job in their own online portal.

For calibration labs, the Metrology Edition adds the depth their work needs.

The Platform

From the First Request to the Paid Invoice

Standard is built on Asset, the core of the platform, fitted to the steps, records and documents of a specific industry. Shown running a calibration lab.

Take a lead from a web form or enter it by hand, qualify it, and move the opportunity across a pipeline board whose stages you set.

Customer Portal

What Your Customers Can Do

Each customer signs in to a portal that shows their own equipment, orders and account.

  1. The Customer’s Overview

    Where their equipment is, their open work orders, what comes due and their account, on one screen.

  2. One Order’s Journey

    Each order shows its steps from opened to closed, and each unit on it shows its own stage.

Request a Quote

Customers choose the equipment and the services, add notes, save a draft and send the request. The business returns a priced quote.

Approve a Quote

Customers approve the quote in the portal, download it, and submit their purchase order number.

Request a Pickup

Customers ask for a pickup at one of their sites with a preferred date, a time window and a note. The request becomes the pickup the business plans, and the customer follows it from requested to collected.

Follow the Work

Each order shows its progress step by step from opened to closed, and each item on the order shows its own stage.

Answer Questions and Decisions

When the business needs an answer before the work can continue, an authorized contact gives it in the portal.

Invoices and Purchase Orders

Customers see each invoice, what is paid and the balance, open the invoice document, and submit a purchase order number.

Message the Business

Customers write to the business from the portal or from the page of a single item. The message opens a conversation with the customer service team.

Equipment and Documents

Customers see their equipment and what is due, and download the documents the business has released.

Store

Customers order from the business’s store, choose shipping or pickup, and the order is billed to their account.

Trust and Security

Offline Field Work
Take a work order’s lines out on a laptop, record readings with no connection, and bring them back: Standard calculates each result on return, and a line changed meanwhile keeps both values for review.
Take Over
A line already out on another laptop moves only by a deliberate Take Over, written into the order’s history; the office can recall a laptop’s lines at any time.
People History
Every change to people, roles and access is kept in a history that cannot be edited.
Walls Between Companies
Each company’s records are walled off from every other company’s by the database itself, and a request that names no company sees nothing.
Sign-In by WorkOS
Sign-in runs on WorkOS, and your administrator connects single sign-on and directory sync to your own identity provider from the People page.
Hosted on AWS
Standard runs in the cloud on Amazon Web Services.
Your Own Server, Your Own Intelligence
No AI is required to run Standard. A company that wants an assistant without sharing its data can have its infrastructure designed around its own server and a local model.
Automation Versions
Compare any version of an automation with the live one, step by step, and restore an earlier version as a draft before it goes live.
