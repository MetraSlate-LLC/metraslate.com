One record

Every Department, the Same Records.

Nothing is copied between the pieces, because there is only one record.

Work and records
Requests, leads and opportunities, estimates, work orders and their lines, each with a lifecycle and a numbered record. An approved estimate converts to a work order in one step, and the records ride the whole chain.
People and permissions
Roles, privileges and qualifications are recorded against the person. Doors check them. The customer portal is a separate face with its own wall.
Equipment and assets
What you own and what your customers own: where it is, what it has been through, what is due, and the documents that belong to it.
Quality and documents
Review before release. Nonconformance, disposition and corrective action as records with owners. Controlled documents with revisions. Documents your business issues are frozen when final and verifiable by whoever holds them.
Reporting
Sigma runs ready reports and lets you compose your own from the same records, with dashboards where the work happens.
Business operations
Invoicing and receipts, credit memos, the books, bank reconciliation and period close, on the same spine as the work that earned the money.

Automation

Your Rules, Running the Building.

Deterministic, readable, changed without a deploy.

Status changes flow through one door. Your published workflows route work through the steps your business requires, notifications go where your rules say, and every move is written to the record with who or what made it. Chat and mail live beside the work, on the record, so a commitment made in a conversation is a record too.

Where it runs

Isolated by Design.

Deployment options and what isolation means here. Hosting.

Each business is its own tenant, and the isolation is enforced in the database itself: a connection without a tenant sees zero rows, not everybody's. Standard deploys as cloud hosting operated by MetraSlate or on hardware you own, with the same platform and the same records either way.
