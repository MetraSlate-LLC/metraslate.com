The proposed experience

A question can become a resource.

The community direction connects discussion with lasting, discoverable knowledge. These are planned capabilities, not a claim that the network is live.

    Ask

  2. 02

    Discuss

    Compare methods, assumptions, and field experience with a defined audience.

  3. 03

    Keep

    Give the useful explanation a home in a library, guide, or shared resource.
