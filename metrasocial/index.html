The community

People, Conversation and Useful Work.

Professional experience and everyday conversation belong in the same community.

People and companies
Build a professional profile around what you do. Find peers, follow their work and learn about the businesses and teams behind it.
Posts and conversations
Ask a technical question, share an observation or start a conversation. Follow discussions that matter to you and save useful contributions.
Channels and messages
Organize conversations around a company, subject or community. Move a discussion into a direct conversation when the audience should be smaller.
Library and wiki
Give useful material a lasting home. Find shared resources, contribute practical knowledge and return to an explanation without hunting through a feed.
Watch and Creator Studio
A place for demonstrations, interviews and educational content, with tools for the people creating and organizing it.

Connected, by choice

A Community Beyond Your Company.

Standard keeps the conversations and records of your business together. MetraSocial serves the wider industry community. A private customer record, internal chat or business document should only leave your company through an explicit sharing decision.

The product direction also includes industry marketplace and creator features. Community access, publishing and moderation come first, so people can participate with clear audience and reporting controls.
