The test definition
The instrument’s datasheet brings test points, tolerances, resolution and formulas to the run. Asset templates describe the model family and its specification.
The measurement policy
Service levels keep the uncertainty, coverage, decision rule, guardband and rounding choices together. The laboratory can review the policy the work uses.
The standards behind it
Connect the standards used to the calibration record. Keep the reference and specification context available when the result is reviewed.
The review
Read the measurement and its basis together before certificate release. Missing information and unresolved conditions need an answer, not an invented result.

Evidence for review

Keep the Record as It Was Completed.

Answer which readings, calculation inputs and decisions supported that result.

Frozen calibration evidence
Completion records the point results, uncertainty components, attributed standards, exclusions and supporting snapshots together. Historical results retain their recorded calculation basis when live reference data changes.
Corrections with a history
Completed calibration events and their result records are append-only at the application database boundary. A correction creates a successor linked to the earlier event, preserving the original rather than silently replacing it.
Review and release evidence
Technician signing, review approval and sealing are separate recorded actions. The laboratory can follow the calibration evidence and the decisions behind certificate release when preparing for an audit.
