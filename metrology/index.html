Metrology Edition

Metrology Edition

The Metrology Edition is the version of Standard made for calibration labs. Metrology is the science of measurement. A calibration lab checks that measuring instruments, such as scales, pressure gauges and thermometers, read correctly, and issues a certificate that proves it. The Metrology Edition records each reading, works out how sure each result is, decides whether the instrument passed, and produces the certificate.
