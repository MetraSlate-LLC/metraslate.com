The Measurement is What the System is Built Around.

In the Metrology Edition the measurement is not something the software stores after the fact. The engine computes the uncertainty, applies the decision rule and produces the verdict in the same transaction that records the reading, and the business runs around that record: from the customer’s request to the sealed certificate to the invoice.

The journey

From Request to Receivable, on One Record.

Six steps, one spine. Nothing is re-typed between them.

  1. 01

    Request and Estimate

    A customer request, from the office or the portal, becomes an estimate priced line by line against the instruments it covers. Approval lands on the estimate with the purchase order number and the note that came with it.

  2. 02

    Work Order

    The approved estimate converts to a work order in one step. Receiving records custody. Lines carry the service level, the assignee and the promise date, and route through the flows your quality system requires.

  3. 03

    Calibration

    The bench works from a structured datasheet. Readings are captured against test points, and the engine computes the uncertainty budget, applies the decision rule and produces the verdict at completion, frozen with the math that produced it.

  4. 04

    Review

    The technician signs, a reviewer approves, and quality seals, with four eyes on every certificate unless your tenant records an audited exception. A stale review refuses; an unapproved signatory refuses.

  5. 05

    Certificate

    The certificate is rendered to PDF/A, sealed and cryptographically signed, and released to the customer. Anyone holding it can verify it online. Amendments supersede; nothing overwrites.

  6. 06

    Invoicing and Receivables

    The invoice is raised from the work order’s lines at the prices the estimate carried, issued once, and paid against, with the books posted inside the same transaction.

Standards and equipment

The Template Carries the Specification.

An instrument is a row in an equipment register until its specification is behind it.

Asset templates
A kind of instrument is described once: its functions, ranges and accuracy specification by range, frequency and option. When that instrument is used as a reference standard, its specification supplies the standard’s contribution to the uncertainty budget.
Datasheets
The unit under test brings its datasheet: test points, tolerances, resolution and formulas, authored once, approved with four eyes, and pinned to the run so a later revision cannot change a record already made.
Specification matching
At each test point the engine selects the assigned standard’s specification by function, role, tier, range and frequency, and records why that band matched, so the budget can be read back.
Traceability
Each standard’s calibration record in force on the day of the work is frozen onto the point, and appears on the certificate with its authority and dates.
Accreditation scope
Calibration and measurement capability is built as a budget and published into the specification catalog, and the printed uncertainty is checked against the published claim.

The math

Exact Decimals, Published Methods.

Nothing in the measurement path passes through floating point.

When a reading is recorded, the engine combines the uncertainty components, computes effective degrees of freedom by Welch-Satterthwaite, takes the coverage factor from a versioned Student’s t-table, and applies the decision rule the service level names. Values are exact decimals from the wire to the database. The engine is checked against published vectors from the GUM and the decision-rule literature, and a disagreement fails the build.

Decision rules
Simple acceptance, the ANSI/NCSL Z540.3 guardband methods, ILAC-G8, ISO 14253-1 and the root-sum-square method, each recorded by name on the point it decided.
Service levels
Guardband method, coverage policy, resolution handling and rounding travel together as a versioned service level, pinned to the run, so the policy is applied by the system rather than remembered at the bench.
Refusal, recorded
A point the engine cannot assess is not guessed. It is recorded as out of verdict with the reason: no matching specification, no standard, no limits, or an unresolved uncertainty.

The artifact

The Certificate is Sealed.

Your customer’s auditor checks the seal, not your word.

When the ceremony completes, the record freezes. The certificate is rendered to PDF/A, sealed, signed and released. Anyone you hand it to can verify it online. Amendments and reseals supersede the earlier document and carry their reason; a withdrawn certificate tells the customer. The vocabulary on the page is versioned so a certificate reads the same on the day it is verified as the day it was issued.

Quality

Review, Nonconformance, Impact.

Quality records have owners, gates and closure conditions your business sets.

Four eyes
The technician signs, a named reviewer approves, quality seals. Signatories are validated against an approved register with a scope.
Nonconformance and disposition
A failed unit raises a disposition. Nonconformances and corrective actions are records with root cause and effectiveness gates your tenant chooses to require.
Impact
When a standard is found out of tolerance, the affected work is traced through the runs that used it and declared as an append-only register, so a recall is a record rather than a search.

Around the bench

Scheduling, Portal, Reporting.

The rest of the business, on the same record.

Scheduling
Work is assigned to people and moves through queues your business defines. Promise dates are carried on the line and shown to the customer.
Offline bench
A run can be checked out to a workstation as a work package, worked without a connection, and drained back. The package carries observations only; the cloud does the math, refuses stale or replayed data, and records conflicts rather than overwriting.
Customer portal
Customers see their own instruments, what is due, released certificates, and their estimates and invoices, scoped to their sites.
Sigma
Ready reports for a calibration business, and reports you compose yourself, from the same records the bench writes.

Composition

How the Edition is Put Together.

The platform underneath is service agnostic. Standard.

Assets, work orders, workflows, portals, documents and reports are the platform’s. The Metrology Edition is what happens when the industry’s own structure is bound into those pieces: an asset becomes a calibratable instrument with a specification behind it, the service level carries the policy that decides the verdict, and the accreditation scope governs what the record is allowed to claim.
