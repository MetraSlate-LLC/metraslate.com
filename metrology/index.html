Metrology Edition

Metrology Edition

The Metrology Edition is Standard for calibration labs: it brings together Asset, the core of the platform, OmniCal Suite’s calculators and worksheets, and the workflows of calibration work. Metrology is the science of measurement. A calibration lab checks that measuring instruments, such as scales, pressure gauges and thermometers, read correctly, and issues a certificate that proves it. The Metrology Edition records each reading, works out how sure each result is, decides whether the instrument passed, and produces the certificate.

The Service Journey

The Job

A reading can be complete while the job waits for review, a customer decision, certificate release or return.

    Customer Request

    Review and Release

  6. 06

    Invoice and Account

    Coordinate shipment and invoicing with the work completed.

Records You Can Trust

Sealed Certificates
Once a certificate is sealed, the database refuses any change or deletion; calibration events and their point results are kept the same way.
Revisions
A correction is a new revision that names the certificate it replaces and states why; the history of issued, replaced and withdrawn stays.
Four Eyes
The technician who calibrated an instrument cannot seal its certificate, and any override the lab allows is recorded where it cannot be edited.
Verify by QR
A QR code on each certificate and label opens a public page showing whether the certificate is current, replaced or withdrawn.
Uncertainty and Decision Rules
Each point’s uncertainty, the standards used and the decision rule with its guard band are calculated and kept with the result.
Capability Before Induction
Each job is checked against the lab’s Scope and capability in real time, before it is received, and goes to the lab that can support it.
Never Better Than Your Scope
A reported uncertainty can never be smaller than the CMC on the lab’s Scope of Accreditation.
