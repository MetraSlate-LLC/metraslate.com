Pilot / Early Access Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition. How Evaluation Works

  1. 01Choose the customer site
  2. 02Prepare selected lines
  3. 03Keep the work package
  4. 04Capture observations
  5. 05Reconcile with the lab
  6. 06Review the completed record

Onsite work starts with the same customer, equipment and service-line identities used by the laboratory. The order names the actual customer site, and the technician takes selected open lines onto a named device with the technical context needed to capture the work.

The field worksheet records observations locally when connectivity is unavailable. Standard calculates uncertainty, TUR and the final decision when the raw values return to the lab, then continues through the same technical review and certificate process.

Preparation makes the disconnected work specific.

The service location is the laboratory or an actual site belonging to the customer. The order can supply that location to its lines, or an individual line can name its own valid service location. The work therefore remains connected to the customer's recorded place of service.

Take Offline selects open work-order lines and checks their readiness and current holder. It prepares one package for the checkout, retaining the pinned technical revisions, measurement grid, limits and standards that remain in calibration through the pass end date. The package's hash is checked when the device receives it.

The worksheet keeps what can be known in the field.

The device must be ready to keep both the application and the work locally before checkout. The technician can then record raw readings, identify the standards used, capture environmental conditions and retain notes against the packaged points.

The worksheet can indicate a reading outside the limits carried with the package. It leaves uncertainty, TUR and the final conformity evaluation to Standard at the lab, where the accepted observations are evaluated against their retained technical basis.

Reconnection asks what the lab actually accepted.

While the application is open, it sends queued work on opening, reconnection or sign-in, and the technician can also choose Send Now. Each capture has its own identity. Resending an item that already landed does not create another observation, and a current reconciliation read reports what became of each item.

The receiving workflow checks the pass, selected line, package and pinned revision. Accepted readings retain their captured timing and synchronization provenance. Conflicting or refused values stay visible for attention, while accepted values remain retained even if a later item cannot be applied.

Completion follows the observations that support it.

A Finish intent is handled after the readings and supporting context. If values in that send were refused, completion waits so the technician can resolve, remove or appropriately exclude the unresolved work before the record is finished.

Once Standard completes the result, the technician can open the finished record. The laboratory can inspect the uncertainty and decision evidence, carry out its technical review and continue to certificate release from the reconciled observations.

Copies and handback keep recovery visible.

This Device distinguishes work not yet sent, values accepted by the lab and values needing attention. Save A Copy preserves unconfirmed work for recovery, while Hand Back checks the lab's current answer before ending the pass.

Work that the lab has not confirmed remains locally retained for 30 days after handback, and the copy provides another way to preserve it. Recall, takeover and a line already finished at the lab are shown as conditions to resolve, rather than a successful send being assumed from connectivity alone.

Manage the whole field pass, including the work that needs recovery.

The field pass begins with selected work, an identified holder and device, a verified package and pinned technical definitions. Its expiry, recall and takeover conditions remain part of the same lifecycle as the observations captured in the field.

Each observation keeps its identity through local capture, transmission and the laboratory's reconciliation. An acknowledgment identifies what was actually accepted. Unsent, refused and conflicting work remains distinguishable, while copies and handback preserve the evidence that still needs attention.

Return accepted field evidence to the normal review path.

Completion follows the accepted observations, standards and required context. Standard evaluates the reconciled inputs and retains the uncertainty and decision evidence with the completed record. The technician and reviewer can then follow the same signing, review, certificate and release responsibilities used for laboratory work.

Reconnection does not erase the difference between a successful send and completed work. The device and laboratory retain their current standing so the technician can resolve an expired pass, a recalled line, a conflicting value or an interrupted transmission without replacing evidence already accepted.
