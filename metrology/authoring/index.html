Automation and reporting

Define the Process. See What Happened.

Build workflows and reports around the same business records.

Event-driven workflows
Choose what starts a workflow, insert actions on the canvas and define conditions, approvals and parallel branches. Test the draft and manage published versions.
Execution history
Inspect the path a run took and the step where work stopped. Keep the drawing and the execution record together when investigating an exception.
Sigma reports
Compose a question from supported records, group the results and compare multiple series. Preview the report, publish it to the intended roles and inspect the contributing records behind supported chart selections.

Smart documents

Your Certificate. Your Template. Your Calibration Data.

Document Creator is the template authoring workspace for the documents your laboratory issues.

Design the certificate once, then populate it from the calibration record. Place your logo, customer and instrument details, measurement results and supporting text on the page. The template controls the presentation; the underlying record supplies the values.

Data-bound fields
Bind document fields to the document type’s supported schema instead of retyping values. Typed bindings distinguish text, dates and other supported field types; validation checks whether the template uses them correctly.
Repeating results tables
Define the columns and formatting once. A repeating table renders the available result rows, so a certificate can show the test points in its calibration record without a separately drawn row for every point.
Page layout and preview
Edit on the page canvas with content and properties together. Control page size, margins, headers, footers and table layout, and inspect a populated preview before publication.
Versioned templates
Keep an editable draft, validate it and publish a template version. Template activation determines which published design is used; publishing a template is separate from reviewing and releasing a customer’s certificate.
