Customer access

More Than a Place to Request a Quote.

The customer portal starts with the account overview, then opens the records behind it.

Equipment and recall
See the customer’s equipment register, calibration dates and upcoming service. Follow each instrument’s recorded location through expected, in-lab, certified and shipped stages.
Work and documents
Follow work-order progress and find the released certificates attached to equipment. View the customer’s quotes and issued invoices within the same account workspace.
Requests and pickups
Request an estimate for selected equipment, describe additional work and include instructions. Ask for a pickup and follow the date and window scheduled by the lab.
A conversation with the lab
Chat With Your Lab stays available from the portal header, alongside the customer’s records and requests.
