Product Strategy

A Broad Foundation. A Deep First Vertical.

Every industry has its own terminology and operating model. Organizations share the need for identity, history, accountability and connected business records. Asset by MetraSlate addresses that common ground. Standard develops it around an industry's specialized processes, records and decisions.

Asset by MetraSlate: The Horizontal Entry Point
Item and material records sit alongside customer work, purchasing, personnel, invoicing and insight. Configured vocabulary lets the organization use its own terms while retaining the shared business foundation.
Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition
The premium first vertical brings calibration, CMC and scope, uncertainty, technical review and certificates into the business workflow. Industry depth creates a distinct buying reason for laboratories.
AssetLight by MetraSlate: Focused Equipment Management
A focused entry point for organizations that need their equipment records and supporting history. Full Asset includes that foundation and adds the wider business scope.

Market Model

A U.S. Opportunity Beyond Calibration.

Asset's scope extends beyond equipment. The model below sizes selected U.S. industries; it does not yet measure the full opportunity across all items, materials and audit-trail use cases.

$2.06B

Illustrative Annual Opportunity in Selected U.S. Industries

View the Market Model, Assumptions and Sensitivity

Source Population. The model uses the U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 County Business Patterns data (new tab): 916,608 establishments with 10 or more employees across 19 nonoverlapping sector groups.

Base Calculation. 916,608 establishments × 25% record/workflow fit × 75% consolidated buyer factor × $12,000 modeled annual account value = $2.062368 billion in illustrative annual license opportunity.

Eligibility Assumptions. The 25% factor estimates fit for record and business workflows. The 75% factor reduces establishments to a modeled buyer population to allow for consolidated purchasing. Both are assumptions about eligibility and buyer structure, not adoption rates. The annual account value is a modeling input.

Sensitivity. Varying record/workflow fit from 10% to 50%, with the other assumptions held constant, produces approximately $825 million to $4.12 billion annually.

Scope. This selected-industry model is not the full Asset TAM or a measured serviceable market, customer forecast or revenue forecast. It makes no global market claim and is not added to a separate laboratory market estimate.
