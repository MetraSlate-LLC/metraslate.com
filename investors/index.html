The Product Foundation

Asset for Business. Standard for Industry.

Asset provides general equipment management and business operations. Standard develops that foundation for an industry’s workflows; Metrology is the first edition.

AssetLight focuses on equipment management. Shared records and business capabilities provide a base for further industry development, with each edition requiring its own domain work and acceptance.

Business Model

Licensing With a Defined Adoption Path.

The commercial model centers on software licensing and scoped onboarding. Product, configuration, users, sites, migration and support determine the proposal; prices and terms are agreed with each customer.

Prove the Buying Reason
Use a laboratory’s representative job to test the connected customer, technical and business workflow. Record the outcome against agreed acceptance criteria.
Make Implementation Repeatable
Establish a migration sample, configure roles and procedures, train operators and agree support responsibilities. Measure the work required to reach a usable deployment.
Learn From Early Adoption
Track evaluation progress, accepted deployments, continued use and implementation effort. These are the operating milestones for building the commercial case.

Development Direction

Expand From Demonstrated Customer Needs.

Other Standard editions are in development or design. Metrologistics is a proposed marketplace connecting equipment owners with providers; it is not an operating revenue stream.

Standard Metrology is offered through scoped demonstrations and pilots. Enabled workflows, integrations, hosting and support require acceptance for the customer’s environment. Read the adoption approach.
