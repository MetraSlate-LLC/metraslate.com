The wedge, and the expansion behind it

One Operating System. An Edition per Industry.

Standard itself is agnostic. Calibration is the wedge we land with; the same operating system expands horizontally from there, one industry at a time.

Read More

Standard itself is agnostic: a shared core of assets, customers, work orders, records and billing that can be shaped to serve any and every industry. Calibration is the wedge we land with, because it is the industry we come from, its records are the hardest to get right, and its buyers cannot tolerate software that guesses. Once the Metrology Edition holds, the same platform expands horizontally, one industry at a time, each edition adopting the specifics of its trade on the same spine.

One industry at a time

The same platform spans from asset management and metrology all the way to solar sales and emergency services dispatching. One shared core, an edition per industry, one industry at a time.
