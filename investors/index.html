Investing in MetraSlate

Enterprise software for equipment
and the businesses that depend on it.

MetraSlate develops enterprise software for equipment-owning organizations and service businesses. Asset manages equipment alongside customer work, purchasing, people, and financial operations. Standard develops that foundation for a specific industry; its Metrology Edition connects calibration, review, certificates, delivery, and invoicing. Asset Light serves organizations that need asset management without the wider business system.

The business problem

A completed measurement
is not a completed job.

Work can still wait on review, a customer decision, a certificate, shipment, or billing. Each handoff affects the service the customer receives and the work the business can finish.

Standard’s Metrology Edition addresses that connected workflow. Asset provides the broader, industry-independent route for organizations managing instrumentation and equipment.

Commercial approach

Build adoption around real work.

Sell the software around the customer’s operation: agree the product, required capabilities, implementation scope, and commercial terms. Asset and Standard provide two routes to the same equipment-centered business market.
