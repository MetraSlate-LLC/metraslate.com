The flywheel

Four Doors into One Company.

A business comes in wherever it needs help first, and the platform carries it the rest of the way around.

AssetThe top of the funnel

A reduced edition of Standard for businesses that own instruments rather than calibrate them. It tracks what is due and pairs each item with a provider on real metrics, right from the coming-due report.

MetrologisticsThe network

The network and bid platform where the job finds the lab that can meet it. Labs join on a free tier and take work before they are ready to migrate their whole business.

StandardThe platform

The full operating system: the calibration itself, the measurement, the certificate, and the whole business around it, from lead to invoice.

MetraSocialThe industry's room

The industry's own room, with premium industry-specific marketing in front of the exact people who buy these services, and a marketplace where the trade buys and sells.

The wedge, and the expansion behind it

Land in Calibration. Expand by Edition.

The same platform spans from asset management and metrology all the way to solar sales and emergency services dispatching.

Standard itself is agnostic: a shared core of assets, customers, work orders, records and billing that can be shaped to serve any industry. Calibration is the wedge we land with, because it is the industry we come from, its records are the hardest to get right, and its buyers cannot tolerate software that guesses.

Once the Metrology Edition holds, the same operating system expands horizontally, one industry at a time, each edition adopting the specifics of its trade on the same spine. The proof carries: a system that satisfies a metrology audit carries any industry's work.
