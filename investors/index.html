The thesis

A Horizontal Platform, Landed Vertically.

The core is agnostic on purpose. The editions are how it enters an industry.

Businesses in every trade run separate systems that never agree, and people re-type numbers between them. Standard removes that seam by being the whole thing. What changes from industry to industry is the vocabulary, the records that cannot be wrong, the standards the work is audited against, and the computations the work requires. That is what an edition is.
