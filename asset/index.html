What It Is

The Metrology Edition, Minus the Bench.

Asset is Standard's Metrology Edition without the parts that complete a calibration.

The measurement engines, the uncertainty and guardband math, and the certificate are not part of it. It tracks your maintenance and the calibratable inventory coming due, and pairs you with providers who can do the calibration, their real metrics on screen, right from the coming due report.

The equipment records, the schedules and the maintenance ship by default. Workflow automation, chat and email, inventory, ERP, CRM, logistics, and Sigma are the same pieces Standard runs on, added as your own tier calls for them rather than bundled in from the start. If you own instruments rather than calibrate them, this is Standard built to your own pace.

The Coming Due Report

From Due to Done, Without Leaving the Report.

The report shows what's coming due. Beside each line, the providers who can do that calibration, matched on capability, with their real metrics on screen: turnaround time, first pass yield, and how fast they answer, each measured from the jobs that ran through the network rather than rated by anybody. You decide right there, and the instrument is on its way. That is Metrologistics by MetraSlate, working inside Asset.

It works with labs on Standard and with labs that aren't. Either way, Asset knows where every instrument is and what came back.
