Asset

Asset is the core of the MetraSlate platform: the equipment, the customers, purchasing, people and invoices that every business in the family runs on. It records what each item is, where it is and what has been done to it, next to the company’s customers, purchasing, staff and invoices.

For companies that own equipment, Asset is the end-to-end business OS, and it plugs directly into Standard, so the work a lab does for them arrives inside their own system.

Standard is Asset fitted to the workflows of a specific industry, and every Standard company runs on it.
