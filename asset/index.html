What it is

Standard, Without the Service Work.

The equipment side of the platform, for businesses that own instruments rather than service them.

The measurement engine, the uncertainty math and the certificate are not part of it. The equipment records, the schedules, the maintenance and the documents are. Workflow automation, chat and mail, inventory, the books, the CRM and Sigma are the same pieces Standard runs on, added as your own tier calls for them rather than bundled in from the start.
