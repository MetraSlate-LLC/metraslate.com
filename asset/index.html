Manage Your Assets. Run Your Business.

Asset is asset management software and end-to-end software for running your business. Know what equipment you own, where it is, who is responsible for it and what needs attention. Connect those records to maintenance, people, purchasing, customer work, invoicing and reporting.

Asset management

Know What You Have. Know What It Needs.

Keep equipment identity, location, responsibility, service dates and history together.

Equipment records
Organize equipment by model, serial number, site and owner. Keep specifications, supporting documents and service history with the individual asset.
Maintenance and service
Plan the work equipment needs, track due dates and record completed service. Use the history to understand recurring problems and upcoming commitments.
Locations and responsibility
See where equipment belongs and who is responsible for it. Keep changes in location and custody connected to its record.

Business operations

Connect the Work Around the Equipment.

Asset management is the starting point. The business does not stop at the equipment register.

Customers and work
Connect customer requirements, quotes and work orders to the assets involved. Keep the agreed scope and responsibility visible as work moves between people.
Purchasing and inventory
Manage the supplies, parts and purchasing behind the work. Connect requirements, orders and receiving to the operation that needs them.
People and communication
Coordinate schedules, responsibilities and conversations around the same work. Give people the context they need to take the next step.
Invoicing and the books
Carry completed work into invoicing and accounts receivable. Connect the commercial record to the customer and the job that produced it.
Automation and reporting
Configure repeatable processes and use Sigma reporting to understand equipment, workload and business performance from connected records.

Asset Light

Asset Management, Without the Business Operations.

Asset Light is Asset without the business operations.

Manage equipment records, locations, custody, service dates, maintenance history and supporting documents. Asset Light focuses on the assets themselves. Asset adds the wider business operations, including customer work, purchasing, invoicing and financial management.

Choose Asset Light when you need equipment management alongside the business systems you already use. Choose Asset when you want to manage the assets and run the business around them.
