When AI takes part in the work,

The Traceability Edition is being designed to record what AI-assisted work was required to do, how it was performed, and how the result was evaluated. It applies Standard’s industry-specialization approach to this domain.

In design

Traceability

A proposed record of requirements, protocols, actions, and evaluation.

This edition is not offered as a currently operational product.

Proposed scope

Make the work inspectable.

The aim is a record of a defined task and its evaluation. It is not a general ranking of AI models.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    What the work was required to produce and the reference it will be evaluated against.

  2. 02

    Controlled protocol

    The versioned procedure followed for this task.

  3. 03

    Attributed actions

    What people and models contributed, in order.

  4. 04

    Evaluation

    How the result was judged and the basis for that decision.

  5. 05

    Evidence of change

    What needs review when a component the work relied on changes.
