Sales and customer accounts

How the workflow operates.

Standard connects leads, opportunities and the sales pipeline to customer accounts, sites, contacts and equipment. Staff can record the service need, owner, stage, expected value and next follow-up, then inspect the actual instrument population and account history behind the opportunity. The same customer context supports quoting, pickup planning and the next calibration cycle.

A service request can become an estimate that identifies the requested work and prices. Standard retains the issued quotation, its revision, the customer's decision and purchase-order context. When approved work becomes a work order, its lines identify the actual instruments and services being delivered. Sales and operations can follow the same agreement instead of rebuilding it from email attachments.

The laboratory can configure service policy at the equipment, site, customer or tenant level. That policy supplies the evaluation context for the work, including the applicable decision and reporting choices. Keeping the selected basis with the job helps the technician understand the requested service and helps a reviewer explain the result later.
