Pilot / Early Access Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition. How Evaluation Works

Pickup Requests

A customer requests collection at a site their portal account is authorized to use. The preferred day, optional collection window and note enter the lab's actual logistics record. An address is required so the request describes a place the driver can reach.

The lab can then confirm operational details, connect equipment and related work where appropriate, and move the stop through its permitted states. The readiness check includes the required map pin and payment-readiness state before dispatch.

Route Planning

A planned route identifies the origin lab, date, driver and stop order. Changing the day also changes the linked stops' scheduled day. Reordering checks that the request includes the route's complete current stop set, and a concurrent mismatch refuses the change instead of leaving a partly rewritten sequence.

A dispatcher can split part of an oversized planned run into another run or change its driver before departure. Once the run has left, the plan is protected from those desk edits so the route the driver is following does not change silently.

Driver View

The company-routes view lets staff select a day, route and stop. A permitted driver can narrow the view to assigned routes. The selected stop shows the customer, address, collection or delivery window, contact details and the next actions available for its current state.

Navigate opens the destination in Google Maps on the device. The route also offers a map view where the map connection is configured, while the ordered stop list remains a direct way to inspect the driving sequence. Route estimates combine returned driving legs with the stated service allowance for each stop.

Handover and Return

Confirming pickup or delivery records who signed for the handover and can retain its supporting note. A failure or cancellation records its reason. Legal transition checks keep a stop from being moved through a state the workflow does not permit.

The movement becomes part of the operating history and can supply a workflow event for the next step. Staff can distinguish a planned collection, a recorded pickup, laboratory receipt, finished calibration and equipment return as different parts of the same service.
