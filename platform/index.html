Standard by MetraSlate

What Standard Helps Your Business Do.

Nine areas of business work in one application. Follow customer requirements into estimates and work orders, manage equipment and people, and connect completed work to documents, invoicing and reporting.

01 of 9

Generate Demand

Manage prospective customers and the work they may bring.

  • Track leads through your configured stages and record when a lead is disqualified.
  • Opportunities move through your stages to the estimate, and a converted lead becomes a customer and an opportunity in one step.
  • Assign customer conversations to the team member handling them, with options to claim, release or transfer a conversation.

02 of 9

Sell and Estimate

Carry customer requirements from an opportunity into the estimate and work order.

  • Price estimates line by line and retain revisions under the same estimate number.
  • Record approval or decline on the estimate, including the customer purchase order number and response notes, from the office or customer portal.
  • Convert an approved estimate to a work order with its agreed line items and prices.

03 of 9

Operate

See the assignment and status of each work order item.

  • Assign work orders and individual items, organize work in queues and retain a history of status changes.
  • Record custody transfers, including who holds the item, when the transfer occurred and who recorded it.
  • Plan pickups, deliveries and routes, and give drivers a view of their scheduled stops.

04 of 9

Equipment and Assets

What you own, and what your customers own.

  • Keep equipment identity, location, service history, documents and due dates together.
  • Templates that describe a kind of equipment once, so each unit inherits its specification.
  • Update service due dates from completed work and the configured service interval.

05 of 9

Quality and Documents

Review before release. Records with owners.

  • Assign owners to nonconformances and corrective actions, record affected-item decisions and follow the review and closure requirements.
  • Controlled documents with revisions, approval and acknowledgement.
  • Documents your business issues are frozen when final, cryptographically sealed, and verifiable by whoever holds them.

06 of 9

Bill and Get Paid

Invoices, payments and financial records connected to the work.

  • Invoices raised from the work order’s lines at the prices the estimate carried, issued once, with tax computed at issue.
  • Apply payments to one or more invoices and record credit memos against the relevant financial documents.
  • The books: chart of accounts, journal, period close, bank statements and reconciliation, connected to the financial documents they record.

07 of 9

Customer Portal

Customer access, connected to the business doing the work.

  • A portal scoped to the customer’s own sites and equipment, their due dates, and the documents released to them.
  • Estimates raised and approved from the portal, on the same record the office sees.
  • Route customer portal conversations to the internal customer-service queue.

08 of 9

People and Access

Who may do what, recorded against the person.

  • Use roles and privileges to control actions, with distinct responsibilities for administrators, operating staff and platform operators.
  • Qualifications and competence recorded against the people who hold them.
  • Scope business records to the organization, with database row-level security supporting that separation.

09 of 9

Automate and Analyze

Define the process, notify the right people and report on the results.

  • Define event, condition and action rules in workflows, then publish updates through the application.
  • Keep notifications and mail associated with the work they concern.
  • Use Sigma to run existing reports, create reports from operational records, display relevant results in dashboards and export them.
