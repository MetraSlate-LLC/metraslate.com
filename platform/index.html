Standard Metrology: calibration and measurement

Build capability budgets, perform manual calibration and preserve the technical basis of the result, in the lab and onsite.

  • Manual calibration

    Standard provides the manual-calibration worksheet, measurement evaluation and technical record for laboratory work.

  • Built-in CMC and scope budgets

    Define the range your laboratory supports, retain the contributors behind it, and connect a published capability curve to later measurement evaluation.

  • Built-in uncertainty evaluation

    Follow the actual standard contributions, resolution, combination, coverage and decision basis from the recorded observation to the reported uncertainty.

  • Measurement-system analysis and calculation tools

    Run Gage R&R, bias, linearity, stability and attribute-agreement studies with their raw data, study design, charts and reports connected to the measurement process.

  • Conditional specifications and instance corrections

    Connect temperature, elapsed time, warm-up, mode, frequency, loading and recorded environmental conditions to controlled specifications and instrument-specific corrections.

  • Calculation and process reference

    Inspect the intended use, inputs, units, assumptions, formula, numerical example and limits of a calculation before applying its result to the laboratory's work.

  • Onsite and offline calibration

    Prepare a device with the right work-order lines, capture raw observations locally and bring them back into Standard's uncertainty, review and certificate workflow.

  • Quality and record control

    Quality belongs to the complete service. Connect findings, technical evidence, corrective actions, controlled documents and customer consequences.

Customers and the service business

Connect the first enquiry, quotation, pickup, work order, customer decision, released certificate and payment.

  • Sales and customer accounts

    Connect leads, opportunities, estimates, approvals and the customer record. Keep the promise visible when the work reaches operations.

  • Customer portal

    Give customers a connected place to plan service, request and approve work, arrange collection, follow their equipment, retrieve released certificates and manage the account with your laboratory.

  • Work orders and connected operations

    Coordinate instrument-level service, collection, receipt, laboratory or onsite work, technical review and return through the same customer and equipment records.

  • Dispatch, routes and equipment return

    Connect customer pickup requests, delivery stops, driving order, destination details and recorded outcomes to the equipment and service behind the trip.

  • Billing and payments

    Set when payment information is required, when an approved amount can be collected and which work may proceed. Keep accepted estimates, approved additions, account coverage and final settlement connected.

The people and equipment behind the work

Give employees the tools to communicate, obtain resources, request support and work across a laboratory network.

  • Chat, email and customer service

    Team chat, connected mail and customer service bring the people responsible into the same working environment as the customer, equipment and service record.

  • Phone and call follow-through

    Bring supported calling into the customer workspace, with authorized account context, observed call history and clear service outcomes.

  • Employee app

    Connect the employee's day, schedule, time, collaboration and internal service requests to the operating records the business already uses.

  • Equipment and tool crib

    Use the shared Asset foundation for equipment identity, location, custody, service history and the resources employees need. Standard carries that foundation into specialized laboratory work.

  • Internal equipment and support

    Use the employee store for equipment and supply requisitions, then connect internal service requests to equipment, priorities, decisions and the people responsible.

  • Company briefings and video

    Publish recorded briefings, training and operational updates for the whole company or a team channel, where staff already work.

  • Multiple labs and corporate management

    Organize locations, regions and people around the operation you actually run, with local working views and a connected company picture.

Control, engineering and improvement

Configure the process, understand operating cost, connect supported systems and choose whether AI belongs in your environment.

  • Workflow automation

    Configure what happens when work changes. Connect operating milestones, financial readiness, notifications and accountable decisions.

  • Analytics and operating cost

    Connect recorded time, materials, purchasing and billing to operational analysis. Explain a result by opening the records behind it.

  • Integrations and technical architecture

    Supported connectors and explicit data contracts connect identity, communication, accounting and measurement evidence with the operational record.

  • Security, identity and access

    Designed with SOC 2 compliance in mind, with organizational identity, company isolation, application permissions and protected connection credentials.

  • Engineering and testing

    Numerical reference cases, controlled-record checks, permission refusals, database integration tests and browser workflows accompany the product from calculation kernels through release preparation.

  • Optional AI and private models

    Run the operation through controlled records, calculations and workflows. Add model assistance where it helps, with a supported model and deployment approach suited to your data-control requirements.
