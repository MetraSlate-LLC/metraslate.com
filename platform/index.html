Standard by MetraSlate

Every Corner of the Business. One Record Spine.

Nine groups of work that most businesses run in disconnected systems, running here as one, around records that are written once and read everywhere.

01 of 9

Generate Demand

A CRM that is part of the system, not bolted onto it.

  • Leads tracked as their own pipeline, in your own stage vocabulary, with disqualification that keeps the pipe honest.
  • Opportunities move through your stages to the estimate, and a converted lead becomes a customer and an opportunity in one step.
  • A customer conversation queue for the people who answer the customer, with claim, release and transfer.

02 of 9

Sell and Estimate

Lead to opportunity to estimate to work order, with nothing re-typed.

  • Estimates priced line by line, revised as a new revision under the same number, so the trail is never lost.
  • Approval or decline lands on the estimate itself, carrying the purchase order number and the note that came with it, from the office or from the customer portal.
  • An approved estimate converts to the work order in one transaction, with the line records riding the whole chain.

03 of 9

Operate

Every line knows where it stands.

  • Work orders and their lines, with assignment, queues, and status changes that all flow through one door and are written to the record.
  • Custody as a ledger: who has the item, since when, and on whose word, recorded rather than inferred.
  • Pickups, deliveries and routes as their own records, with a driver’s day laid out for the person driving it.

04 of 9

Equipment and Assets

What you own, and what your customers own.

  • A register of equipment with its location, its history of events, its documents and its schedule on the same record the work touches.
  • Templates that describe a kind of equipment once, so each unit inherits its specification.
  • Service due dates that advance from the work itself, with the basis recorded.

05 of 9

Quality and Documents

Review before release. Records with owners.

  • Nonconformance, disposition and corrective action as records with owners, gates and closure conditions your business sets.
  • Controlled documents with revisions, approval and acknowledgement.
  • Documents your business issues are frozen when final, cryptographically sealed, and verifiable by whoever holds them.

06 of 9

Bill and Get Paid

The money side rides the same spine.

  • Invoices raised from the work order’s lines at the prices the estimate carried, issued once, with tax computed at issue.
  • Receipts recorded against invoices, one payment across several, credit memos as the mirror of an issue.
  • The books: chart of accounts, journal, period close, bank statements and reconciliation, with every posting inside the document’s own transaction.

07 of 9

Face the Customer

Your customers see a business that has it together.

  • A portal scoped to the customer’s own sites and equipment, their due dates, and the documents released to them.
  • Estimates raised and approved from the portal, on the same record the office sees.
  • Customer service chat from inside the portal, landing in the queue that answers it.

08 of 9

People and Access

Who may do what, recorded against the person.

  • Roles and privileges enforced at the door, with administrators, operating staff and platform operators kept distinct.
  • Qualifications and competence recorded against the people who hold them.
  • Each business is its own tenant, isolated in the database itself, failing closed to zero rows.

09 of 9

Automate and Analyze

Your rules, running the building.

  • A workflow engine with plain event, condition and action rules, published as flows and changed without a deploy.
  • Notifications and mail on the record, beside the work they concern.
  • Sigma: ready reports and reports you compose yourself, with dashboards where the work happens and exports when a number has to travel.
