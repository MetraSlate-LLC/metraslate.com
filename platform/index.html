Sell & Estimate

The seamless handoff: lead to opportunity to estimate to work order.

  • Opportunities tracked through your own stages to the estimate.
  • Estimates priced line by line against the assets they cover, revised without losing the trail.
  • Approval or rejection lands on the estimate itself, carrying the PO number and the note that came with it.
  • A won estimate converts to the work order, with the asset records riding the whole chain. Nothing is re-typed.

Operate

Every line knows where it stands.

  • Work orders intake with the payment method attached from the start.
  • Lines route through your published service flows: bench, QA, and beyond.
  • QA failures are recorded with step, user and severity, and the customer decides the disposition in the loop.
  • Repair work carries its parts list and price-at-use, so the bill is never a surprise.
  • The equipment register rides beside the work: every asset's history, documents and schedule on the same record the job touches.
  • Pickup and delivery run as their own dispatch board: routes, stops and live driver position on one map, tied back to the work order they're carrying.

Measure

The datasheet drives the bench.

  • Structured datasheets with test groups and points, authored once.
  • Spec curve to uncertainty to TUR to guardband to verdict, computed at capture.
  • CMC budgets built on the A2LA R205 methodology, pushed straight into your specs.
  • Scope of accreditation managed in the same system that does the work.

Certify

The record is the evidence.

  • Certificates seal and sign cryptographically the moment work completes.
  • Revisions supersede, never overwrite, with a cosmetic-only allowlist.
  • Anyone downstream verifies a certificate independently, online, instantly.

Bill & Get Paid

The money side rides the same spine.

  • Charges accumulate as work happens and roll up into invoices with your pricing rules.
  • Payments record against the invoice, with the amount paid and the balance left standing on it.
  • Balances age against their own terms, so you know where you actually stand.

Face the Customer

Your customers see a business that has it together.

  • A portal scoped to their own assets, their due dates and their certificates.
  • Customer service chat from inside the portal, landing in the queue that answers it.
  • Requests raised against their own equipment, priced back as a real estimate.
  • Payment on their own invoices, in the same place they read them.

Govern

Control that holds up in an audit.

  • Roles split cleanly between operating staff, admins and platform operators.
  • Technician qualifications enforced with warn-and-block hooks at the work itself.
  • Calibration interval optimization with a human in the loop and full provenance.
  • Multi-tenant isolation enforced in the database kernel, failing closed to zero rows.

Automate & Analyze

Your rules, running the building.

  • A workflow engine with two dozen action types across the whole lifecycle.
  • Plain event-condition-action rules compiled into flows, changed without a deploy.
  • Reports and dashboards built from your own vocabulary, no SQL in sight.
  • Scheduled deliverables compiled and sent on your cadence.
