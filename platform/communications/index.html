Pilot / Early Access Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition. How Evaluation Works

Email and calling require supported provider accounts and configuration. A saved draft, attempted send and confirmed provider outcome remain distinct.

Team channels, direct messages and customer-service conversations let staff coordinate around the account and work. Threads, mentions, pinned messages and files keep useful context available.

Customer-service queues identify the conversation, responsible representative and waiting time. Staff can accept, transfer and close a conversation; access to linked records still follows each person’s permissions.

Team and Customer Conversations

A message about an instrument is more useful when the instrument has a clear identity. Related-record links let staff name relevant records in their internal discussion, while server-side checks control which records a writer may include. Customer-service conversations keep the customer's access separate from the laboratory's internal record links.

The practical benefit is a cleaner handoff. A colleague can read the discussion, identify the relevant work and continue the next action with the record in view.

Connected Email

An email draft belongs to the connected account, and supported read, archive, trash and attachment actions follow that provider. Mailbox ownership is checked before access. Staff can work from Standard while retaining the account's own correspondence and provider controls.

Sending has an identifiable outcome. A provider refusal is recorded so a representative can see that an attempted message did not succeed. A released document and successful notification remain separate facts.
