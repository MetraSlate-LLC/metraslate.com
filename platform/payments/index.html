Quotations, customer decisions, invoices and recorded receipts provide the commercial record around a service. A purchase order or accepted quotation records an agreement; it does not mean money has been received.

Newer agreement controls retain the reviewed estimate revision, approved subset and terms. Their availability must be confirmed against the pilot configuration. Live collection, card holds, automatic charging, refunds and customer self-payment are not active in the offer described here.

An Approval Has a Specific Basis

The quotation gives the customer a defined service and price to review. Revised work needs its own clear decision. Retaining the agreement helps staff explain what was approved when preparing the invoice.

The Invoice Explains the Account Position

Invoice lines, recorded receipts and balances belong with the customer and work they concern. Review access and the accounting process during setup so people know which system records each fact.

Payment Automation Is a Separate Development

Configurable funding requirements, card authorizations, provider collection, refunds and self-payment need additional activation and acceptance. They are not implied by the presence of an invoice, approval or payment record. Bring these requirements to the pilot discussion.
