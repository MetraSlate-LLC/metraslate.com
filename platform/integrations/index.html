Customer conversations are easier to handle when the relevant records are close at hand. Connect the accounts your team uses for email and calling, and give each person access to the work they are responsible for.

Onboarding brings your provider accounts, sign-in requirements and team permissions together. Your organization chooses the connections that fit its operation.

Email Where the Work Happens

Open your connected mailbox, read messages and attachments, and compose replies while working with customer and service records. Mailbox connections keep each person’s email account associated with its owner.

Sign-In That Fits Your Organization

Configure organization sign-in and team membership during onboarding. Roles and permissions give employees access to the records and actions they need for their work.

Customer Context for Every Call

With your RingCentral account configured, staff can identify the caller, open the relevant customer account and use the connected call controls. Call history and recorded outcomes help the next person continue the conversation.

Set Up the Connections Your Team Uses

Bring your email, identity and calling requirements to onboarding. We work through account setup, access and the everyday tasks your team will use, so the connections fit your operation.
