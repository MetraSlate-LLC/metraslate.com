Pilot / Early Access Standard by MetraSlate: Metrology Edition. How Evaluation Works

Configure your organization’s RingCentral account and calling access during onboarding.

The phone experience is designed around the account the caller needs help with. The RingCentral integration path connects the embedded provider experience to Standard's authorized caller lookup, allowing the representative to identify the customer and inspect the relevant equipment, quotation or work while speaking.

The provider connection supplies calling. Standard's embedded boundary supports incoming and outgoing session events, dialing and the applicable answer, hold, resume, transfer and hangup controls. Provider registration, account authorization and the deployed media environment establish the actual calling setup.

The workspace keeps call control and service responsibility together. Staff can work from the customer-service conversation queue, use the lab directory, inspect previous calls and record the result of an ended conversation. A customer can receive a short feedback request through the configured survey channel and respond through the portal.

Call history retains observed timing and account context. Reports can show completed calls, answer timing, accumulated hold, representative dispositions, first-contact review coverage and submitted customer feedback. Organization, region, lab/site and representative views help management understand where service needs attention.

Each measure keeps its meaning. First-contact resolution is based on an explicit representative outcome and first-contact answer; customer-confirmed resolution comes from submitted surveys. Missing eligible data stays unknown. Client-observed events may omit calls made outside the connected workspace or while the connection was unavailable.

Configure the RingCentral connection, authorize the organization’s account and bring calling into the customer workspace. The laboratory keeps its account, equipment and work records alongside the conversation.

Account and Call History

The customer may be calling about a delayed pickup, a failed instrument, a quotation or a released certificate. Account context lets the representative look at the actual service record and continue the conversation from an identifiable fact.

Call history and disposition then preserve what the team observed and decided. The next person can see the account, the previous contact and the follow-through still required.

Service Measures

A short answer time and a resolved question describe different outcomes. Standard separates observed call timing, representative-reported resolution, review coverage and submitted customer feedback so a manager can inspect each on its own terms.

The reports identify their source as client-observed events. Calls outside the connected workspace or missed while disconnected may be absent, so the figures should be read as service evidence from that connection.
