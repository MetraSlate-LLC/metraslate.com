Editions

One Platform. An Edition per Industry.

The platform holds what every business has in common: work and the records it leaves, people and what they may do, equipment, quality and documents, reporting, and the business side. An edition adds the workflows, terminology and technical requirements of a particular industry. Metrology adds calibration and measurement evidence; Public Safety focuses on agency operations and equipment readiness.

The editions

Where Each One Stands.

Three editions, in the order they are being delivered, with their stage stated plainly. Each edition’s document is linked as issued; the whole set is in the library.

Metrology EditionThe first edition
Calibration and metrology businesses: the request, the estimate, the work order, the calibration itself, the review, the sealed certificate, and the invoice, on one record. See the Metrology Edition.

Public Safety EditionNext, in development
Agency operations: personnel and their qualifications, equipment readiness, review and quality, and the records an agency is obliged to keep, alongside the emergency communications systems it already runs. See where it stands.

A defensible record of AI-assisted work: requirements, controlled protocols, recorded actions, evaluation, and evidence of change, on the same sealed record spine. See the Traceability Edition.

Common to all

What Does Not Change Between Editions.

Customer and work records, access controls, documents and reporting remain connected across the platform.

The record
Keep numbered records, status, ownership and change history with the work.
Permissions
Roles and privileges are recorded against the person and enforced at the door, the same way in every edition.
Documents
Documents a business issues are frozen when they are final. A recipient can verify a sealed document without calling the issuer.
Reporting
Sigma answers questions from the same records the work is done on, in every edition.
Tenancy
Each organization is its own tenant, isolated in the database itself, not only in the application.
